**Meaningful white space (and why):**

- **Signalling × pre/post mandate (G1/G6).** A mandate turns some formerly discretionary items into compulsory ones. Signalling predicts their price effect should **fall** after the mandate; information-asymmetry theory predicts it should **persist**. This is one of the few cells where two theories in the corpus make **opposite, testable** predictions.
- **Legitimacy × sensitive industries (G4).** Legitimacy theory predicts reactive, soft disclosure by polluters is discounted. Signalling predicts hard, quantified disclosure is rewarded. C1 sits exactly here.
- **Soft budget constraint × SOE (G9).** Opposite-signed controls in P2 and P5 plus conflicting cited international evidence. Meaningful, but best treated as a moderator.
- **Legitimacy × stress = X.** No coherent prediction about legitimacy dynamics in a short-run stress window, so this empty cell is not a gap.
